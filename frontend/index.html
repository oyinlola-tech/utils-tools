Local processing

Your files never leave your machine.

Utils-tool runs on your computer. Files are processed by the local server, stored only in a temporary directory, and cleaned up automatically after each job. No uploads, no tracking.

No account Nothing to sign up for. Just open the app and work.
No database Temporary state lives on disk, then disappears.
No cloud upload All processing happens on your machine.
Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What is Utils-tool?
A free, local-first collection of file utilities. Compress, convert, edit, remove backgrounds, process PDFs and analyze files.
Does Utils-tool work offline?
Yes. When started locally, all processing and storage happen on your computer — no internet connection is required.
Where are my files stored?
Uploaded files are written to a temporary local directory while being processed and are cleaned up automatically.
What file formats are supported?
Images (JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF), PDFs, SVG, ZIP and other common types. The Video Downloader supports YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram and 1000+ sites. Each tool lists the exact formats it accepts.
Can I download videos without watermarks?
Yes. The Video Downloader tool uses yt-dlp under the hood to fetch the highest-quality source stream — no watermark, no re-encoding. Paste a URL and choose the format you want.
How does Text-to-Speech work?
Type or paste your text, choose a language, and the tool generates an MP3 audio file using Google TTS. The file is downloaded straight to your device — nothing is stored on the server.
Can I process multiple files at once?
Yes — most tools support batch processing. The per-tool page shows the exact limit.
Is Utils-tool free?
Yes. There are no paid tiers and no account is required.

Ready to get started?

No account. No subscriptions. Just fast, private tools in your browser.
